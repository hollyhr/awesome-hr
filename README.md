# Awesome HR

A curated collection of useful, maintained resources for people operations,
employment practice and HR technology—with an emphasis on UK teams and open
developer standards.

This is a practical starting point, not legal advice or a directory of every HR
vendor. Links are selected for authority, usefulness and maintenance.

## UK employment essentials

- [Acas](https://www.acas.org.uk/) — free, impartial workplace-rights and
  employment-practice guidance for employers and workers.
- [Employ people — GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/browse/business/employ-people) —
  the government index for hiring, contracts, payroll, leave and ending
  employment.
- [Employment status and rights checklist](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/employment-status-and-employment-rights/employment-status-and-rights-checklist-for-employers-and-other-engagers)
  — a current employer checklist for distinguishing employees, workers and
  self-employed people.
- [Employ someone: step by step](https://www.gov.uk/employ-someone) — the
  official route through pay, checks, contracts, insurance and registration.
- [Check employment status for tax](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/check-employment-status-for-tax)
  — HMRC's status tool and guidance for workers and engagements.
- [DBS checks for employers](https://www.gov.uk/dbs-check-applicant-criminal-record)
  — when and how an employer can request a criminal-record check.
- [Preventing discrimination when employing people](https://www.gov.uk/employer-preventing-discrimination)
  — protected characteristics and fair recruitment responsibilities.
- [Written statements of employment particulars](https://www.gov.uk/employment-contracts-and-conditions/written-statement-of-employment-particulars)
  — the information employers must give workers and employees.
- [Holiday entitlement](https://www.gov.uk/holiday-entitlement-rights) —
  statutory leave rules and links to the official calculator.
- [Flexible working](https://www.gov.uk/flexible-working) — statutory request
  rules for employees and employer decision duties.
- [Maternity pay and leave](https://www.gov.uk/maternity-pay-leave) — statutory
  eligibility, notice, leave and pay guidance.
- [Paternity pay and leave](https://www.gov.uk/paternity-pay-leave) — statutory
  eligibility, notice, leave and pay guidance.
- [Shared Parental Leave and Pay](https://www.gov.uk/shared-parental-leave-and-pay)
  — eligibility and booking rules for eligible parents.
- [Carer's Leave](https://www.gov.uk/carers-leave) — the statutory unpaid-leave
  entitlement for employees with a dependant needing long-term care.
- [Statutory Sick Pay employer guide](https://www.gov.uk/employers-sick-pay) —
  current eligibility, rates and employer responsibilities.
- [PAYE and payroll for employers](https://www.gov.uk/paye-for-employers) —
  HMRC's guide to records, reporting, deductions and payments.
- [National Minimum Wage calculator for employers](https://www.gov.uk/minimum-wage-calculator-employers)
  — the official check for minimum-wage compliance.
- [Check a job applicant's right to work](https://www.gov.uk/check-job-applicant-right-to-work)
  — the official online and document-check routes.
- [The Pensions Regulator: automatic enrolment](https://www.thepensionsregulator.gov.uk/en/employers)
  — employer duties and guidance.

## Managing people fairly

- [Acas templates](https://www.acas.org.uk/templates) — free letters, forms,
  policies and process templates for employers and workers.
- [Acas Code of Practice on disciplinary and grievance procedures](https://www.acas.org.uk/acas-code-of-practice-on-disciplinary-and-grievance-procedures)
  — the recognised minimum standard for handling workplace cases fairly.
- [Flexible working guidance](https://www.acas.org.uk/flexible-working) —
  practical Acas guidance for requests, consultation and decisions.
- [Managing staff redundancies](https://www.acas.org.uk/manage-staff-redundancies)
  — a structured route through planning, consultation, selection and support.
- [Reasonable adjustments at work](https://www.acas.org.uk/reasonable-adjustments)
  — guidance for discussing, agreeing and reviewing adjustments.
- [Employment statutory code of practice](https://www.equalityhumanrights.com/equality/equality-act-2010/codes-practice/employment-statutory-code-practice)
  — Equality and Human Rights Commission guidance on applying equality law at
  work.
- [HSE Management Standards for work-related stress](https://www.hse.gov.uk/stress/standards/)
  — a framework for identifying and managing common workplace stressors.

## Privacy, safety and accessibility

- [ICO employment information](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/employment/)
  — UK GDPR guidance for employers handling worker information.
- [ICO small-business data protection hub](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations/)
  — plain-language privacy guidance and tools.
- [HSE: managing health and safety](https://www.hse.gov.uk/simple-health-safety/)
  — practical health-and-safety guidance for smaller organisations.
- [HSE risk assessment](https://www.hse.gov.uk/simple-health-safety/risk/)
  — a practical process and templates for controlling workplace risks.
- [NCSC small-business cyber security guide](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/small-business-guide)
  — accessible actions for protecting accounts, devices and business data.
- [Cyber Essentials](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/cyberessentials/overview) — the
  UK-backed baseline certification scheme for common cyber risks.
- [W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/)
  — the core accessibility standard for employee-facing software.
- [W3C easy accessibility checks](https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/preliminary/)
  — a first review of common web-accessibility barriers.
- [OWASP API Security Top 10](https://owasp.org/API-Security/) — common API
  risks relevant to systems containing employee data.

## Skills and workforce development

- [Employing an apprentice](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-take-on-an-apprentice)
  — official guidance on contracts, training, funding and employer duties.
- [National Careers Service skills assessment](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/skills-assessment)
  — a free starting point for reflecting on skills and suitable work.
- [Open Skills Network](https://www.openskillsnetwork.org/) — work on open,
  machine-actionable skills data and shared tools.
- [Rich Skill Descriptors](https://www.openskillsnetwork.org/rsd) — a portable
  structure for giving skills definition, context and connections.

## Open-source HR software

- [Frappe HR](https://github.com/frappe/hrms) — HR and payroll software built
  on the Frappe framework; GPL-3.0.
- [OrangeHRM](https://github.com/orangehrm/orangehrm) — community HR management
  system; GPL-3.0.
- [Horilla HR](https://github.com/horilla/horilla-hr) — Django-based HR
  software; LGPL-2.1.
- [Odoo](https://github.com/odoo/odoo) — broad business application suite with
  HR modules; check the licence of the edition and add-ons you use.

Inclusion here is not an endorsement. Review security posture, release activity,
licensing, regional payroll assumptions and operational ownership before
adopting any HR system.

## Standards for HR integrations

- [OpenAPI Specification](https://spec.openapis.org/oas/latest.html) —
  language-neutral HTTP API contracts.
- [SCIM core schema — RFC 7643](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7643)
  and [SCIM protocol — RFC 7644](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7644)
  — interoperable identity provisioning.
- [CloudEvents](https://cloudevents.io/) — a common event-envelope
  specification.
- [OpenID Connect](https://openid.net/developers/how-connect-works/) — identity
  on top of OAuth 2.0.
- [WebAuthn](https://www.w3.org/TR/webauthn-3/) — public-key credentials and
  passkeys.
- [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/) —
  an open protocol for exposing tools and context to compatible AI clients.
- [HR Open Standards](https://www.hropenstandards.org/) — interoperable data
  specifications for recruiting, employment and talent systems.
- [1EdTech Open Badges](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/open-badges) — a
  verifiable standard for portable achievements and credentials.
- [1EdTech Comprehensive Learner Record](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/clr)
  — an interoperable record for skills, competencies and achievements.
- [OAuth 2.0 Security Best Current Practice — RFC 9700](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9700.html)
  — current security guidance for OAuth clients and authorisation servers.
- [Protected Resource Metadata — RFC 9728](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9728.html)
  — discovery metadata for OAuth-protected APIs and MCP servers.
- [Standard Webhooks](https://www.standardwebhooks.com/) — an open set of
  conventions for signing and verifying webhook deliveries.

## Developer resources

- [HollyHR developer docs](https://developers.hollyhr.com) — REST API, OpenAPI,
  TypeScript SDK, webhooks and hosted MCP from this list's maintainers.
- [HollyHR API examples](https://github.com/hollyhr/hollyhr-api-examples) —
  runnable read, pagination, safe-write, webhook and MCP examples.
- [HollyHR TypeScript API client](https://github.com/hollyhr/hollyhr-api-client)
  — public SDK source, versioned OpenAPI contract and releases.
- [Official MCP Registry](https://registry.modelcontextprotocol.io/) — public
  discovery metadata for published MCP servers.
- [AsyncAPI](https://www.asyncapi.com/docs) — contracts and tooling for
  event-driven and message-based APIs.
- [OpenTelemetry](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/) — vendor-neutral telemetry
  APIs and conventions for traces, metrics and logs.
- [JSON Schema](https://json-schema.org/) — portable validation contracts used
  by OpenAPI and many event systems.
- [OWASP Cheat Sheet Series](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/) — concise
  implementation guidance for authentication, authorisation, secrets and other
  security boundaries.

## Contributing

Please read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before proposing a link.
Additions should be:

- directly useful to HR operators or people-technology builders;
- maintained and linked to their authoritative source;
- clear about geography, licence and commercial status where relevant;
- free of referral codes, affiliate links and unverifiable superlatives.

Commercial tools are considered only when their documentation or open
specification is independently useful. This list will not become a pay-to-play
vendor directory.

Paul Gould (`@paulgould`) is the accountable maintainer. The review cadence,
quality bar, growth target and continuity rules are recorded in
[MAINTENANCE.md](MAINTENANCE.md).
