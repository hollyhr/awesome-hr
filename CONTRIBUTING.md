# Contributing

Thank you for helping keep Awesome HR useful.

## Propose a link

Open a pull request that:

1. Adds one link in the most relevant existing section.
2. Uses the authoritative project, regulator or specification URL.
3. Describes what the resource provides without marketing language.
4. States an open-source licence or regional limitation when it matters.
5. Keeps entries alphabetic within their natural subgroup where practical.

Please explain why the resource is useful and confirm that you checked the link.
Use the pull-request template so reviewers can verify authority, maintenance and
commercial independence consistently.

## Review standard

Each entry should pass all of these checks:

- **Useful:** solves a recognisable problem for an HR operator, employer,
  worker or people-technology builder.
- **Authoritative:** links to the regulator, standards body, project owner or
  original maintainer rather than a summary or search result.
- **Current:** has a credible maintainer and is not superseded, abandoned or
  materially misleading.
- **Specific:** says what the reader will find and identifies important UK,
  licence or commercial limitations.
- **Independent:** contains no affiliate tracking, paid placement or disguised
  promotion.

Commercial vendors may propose public specifications, developer documentation
or genuinely reusable research. Product-directory entries and generic vendor
homepages remain out of scope.

## What we do not accept

- affiliate, referral or tracking URLs;
- paid placement;
- abandoned projects presented as current;
- generic vendor homepages with no independently useful documentation;
- copied articles, legal advice or unverified compliance claims;
- duplicate resources that add no meaningful coverage.

Maintainers may decline additions to keep the list focused.

## Maintenance changes

Broken-link removals, replacements and factual corrections are as valuable as
new entries. For a replacement, explain why the new source is more current or
authoritative. Security concerns should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a
public issue.
