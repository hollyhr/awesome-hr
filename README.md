## UK employment law

- [Acas](https://www.acas.org.uk/) - Free, impartial guidance on workplace rights and employment practice for employers and workers.
- [Employ people](https://www.gov.uk/browse/business/employ-people) - The GOV.UK index for hiring, contracts, payroll, leave and ending employment.
- [Employ someone: step by step](https://www.gov.uk/employ-someone) - The official route through pay, checks, contracts, insurance and registration for a first hire.
- [Implementing the Plan to Make Work Pay and Employment Rights Act](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/implementing-the-plan-to-make-work-pay-and-employment-rights-act) - The government's timeline for when each Employment Rights Act 2025 change takes effect.
- [Employment status and rights checklist](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/employment-status-and-employment-rights/employment-status-and-rights-checklist-for-employers-and-other-engagers) - Helps employers tell employees, workers and self-employed people apart.
- [Check employment status for tax](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/check-employment-status-for-tax) - HMRC's status tool and guidance for individual workers and engagements.
- [Preventing discrimination when employing people](https://www.gov.uk/employer-preventing-discrimination) - Protected characteristics and fair recruitment responsibilities.
- [Check a job applicant's right to work](https://www.gov.uk/check-job-applicant-right-to-work) - The official online and document-check routes.
- [DBS checks for employers](https://www.gov.uk/dbs-check-applicant-criminal-record) - When and how an employer can request a criminal record check.
- [Written statements of employment particulars](https://www.gov.uk/employment-contracts-and-conditions/written-statement-of-employment-particulars) - The information employers must give workers and employees.
- [PAYE and payroll for employers](https://www.gov.uk/paye-for-employers) - HMRC's guide to payroll records, reporting, deductions and payments.
- [National Minimum Wage calculator for employers](https://www.gov.uk/minimum-wage-calculator-employers) - The official check that pay meets the minimum wage.
- [The Pensions Regulator: employers](https://www.thepensionsregulator.gov.uk/en/employers) - Automatic enrolment duties and how to meet them.
- [Statutory Sick Pay employer guide](https://www.gov.uk/employers-sick-pay) - Eligibility, current rates and what employers have to do.
- [Holiday entitlement](https://www.gov.uk/holiday-entitlement-rights) - Statutory leave rules, with a link to the official calculator.
- [Maternity pay and leave](https://www.gov.uk/maternity-pay-leave) - Statutory eligibility, notice, leave and pay.
- [Paternity pay and leave](https://www.gov.uk/paternity-pay-leave) - Statutory eligibility, notice, leave and pay.
- [Shared Parental Leave and Pay](https://www.gov.uk/shared-parental-leave-and-pay) - Eligibility and booking rules for parents sharing leave.
- [Carer's Leave](https://www.gov.uk/carers-leave) - Unpaid leave for employees with a dependant who needs long-term care.
- [Flexible working](https://www.gov.uk/flexible-working) - The legal rules for making and deciding a flexible working request.

## UK workplace practice

- [Acas templates](https://www.acas.org.uk/templates) - Free letters, forms, policies and process templates.
- [Acas Code of Practice on disciplinary and grievance procedures](https://www.acas.org.uk/acas-code-of-practice-on-disciplinary-and-grievance-procedures) - The recognised minimum standard for handling workplace cases fairly.
- [Acas flexible working guidance](https://www.acas.org.uk/flexible-working) - Practical advice on handling requests well, beyond the legal minimum.
- [Managing staff redundancies](https://www.acas.org.uk/manage-staff-redundancies) - Planning, consultation, selection and support, step by step.
- [Reasonable adjustments at work](https://www.acas.org.uk/reasonable-adjustments) - How to discuss, agree and review adjustments with disabled staff.
- [Acas: Employment Rights Act 2025](https://www.acas.org.uk/employment-rights-act-2025) - What has already changed under the Act, what is still to come and when, with links to Acas advice on each change.
- [Employment statutory code of practice](https://www.equalityhumanrights.com/equality/equality-act-2010/codes-practice/employment-statutory-code-practice) - The Equality and Human Rights Commission's guide to applying equality law at work.
- [HSE Management Standards for work-related stress](https://www.hse.gov.uk/stress/standards/) - A framework for spotting and managing common causes of stress at work.

## UK privacy and safety

- [ICO employment guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/employment/) - UK GDPR guidance for employers handling information about workers.
- [ICO advice for small organisations](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-for-small-organisations/) - Plain-language data protection guidance and tools.
- [HSE: managing health and safety](https://www.hse.gov.uk/simple-health-safety/) - Health and safety basics for smaller organisations.
- [HSE risk assessment](https://www.hse.gov.uk/simple-health-safety/risk/) - A simple process and templates for controlling workplace risks.
- [NCSC small business guide](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/small-business-guide) - Practical steps for protecting accounts, devices and business data.
- [Cyber Essentials](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/cyberessentials/overview) - The government-backed baseline certification for common cyber risks.

## UK skills and apprenticeships

- [National Careers Service skills assessment](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/skills-assessment) - A free starting point for reflecting on skills and suitable work.

## Accessibility and security

- [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/) - The W3C accessibility standard, worth applying to careers pages and staff-facing tools.
- [Easy checks for web accessibility](https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/preliminary/) - A first W3C review of common accessibility barriers.
- [OWASP API Security Top 10](https://owasp.org/API-Security/) - The most common API risks, relevant to any system holding employee data.

## Open-source HR software

- [Frappe HR](https://github.com/frappe/hrms) - HR and payroll software built on the Frappe framework (GPL-3.0).
- [Horilla HR](https://github.com/horilla/horilla-hr) - Django-based HR software (LGPL-2.1).
- [Odoo](https://github.com/odoo/odoo) - Business application suite with HR modules. Licences differ between the Community and Enterprise editions.
- [OrangeHRM](https://github.com/orangehrm/orangehrm) - Community HR management system (GPL-3.0).

## HR data standards

- [SCIM core schema (RFC 7643)](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7643) - The standard schema for users and groups, used to provision accounts from HR systems.
- [SCIM protocol (RFC 7644)](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7644) - The HTTP protocol for creating, updating and removing those accounts.
- [HR Open Standards](https://www.hropenstandards.org/) - Shared data specifications for recruiting, employment and talent systems.
- [1EdTech Open Badges](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/open-badges) - A verifiable standard for portable achievements and credentials.
- [1EdTech Comprehensive Learner Record](https://www.1edtech.org/standards/clr) - A shareable record of skills, competencies and achievements.
- [Open Skills Network](https://www.openskillsnetwork.org/) - A coalition working on open, machine-readable skills data.
- [Rich Skill Descriptors](https://www.openskillsnetwork.org/rsd) - A portable format that gives each skill a definition, context and links to related skills.
